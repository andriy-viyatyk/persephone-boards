Vendored third-party libraries for the Excel Viewer board.

SheetJS (xlsx) — parser
  version: 0.20.3
  license: Apache-2.0
  file:    xlsx.full.min.js  (standalone build, no dependencies)
  source:  https://cdn.sheetjs.com/xlsx-0.20.3/package/dist/xlsx.full.min.js
  role:    reads .xlsx (OOXML) and legacy .xls (BIFF) binary into arrays

av-grid — renderer
  version: 2.1.0
  license: MIT
  files:   av-grid.umd.js (renamed from the package's av-grid.umd.cjs), av-grid.css
  source:  npm `av-grid` → dist/av-grid.umd.cjs + dist/av-grid.css
           https://github.com/andriy-viyatyk/av-grid
  role:    grid renderer — virtualized rendering, sorting, filtering, range select,
           clipboard copy, context menu. Themed straight off the --p-* contract, so
           it needs no skin file.

See LICENSE for the full Apache-2.0 (SheetJS) and MIT (av-grid) texts.
