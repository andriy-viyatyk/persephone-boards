# Gem Keep

## 1.0.0

- First version: a 3D tower-defense game with three levels, four tower types with upgrades, four enemy types and a meteor spell. Zoom with the mouse wheel; rotate and pan the camera with the mouse, a pen or touch.
- Sound effects for towers, hits, explosions, meteors, waves, building and upgrades, plus soft wind and win/lose jingles; mute with the speaker button, the M key or the tab's mute button.
- Raiders steal gems from the vault plate and carry them back to their portal; a gem is lost only when it goes through. A killed carrier drops its gems on the road, and the next raider passing by picks them up.
- Easier balance: stronger towers, bigger bounties, more starting gold and mana, and a gentle first wave.
- Raiders are now animated monsters (ninjas, orcs, yetis and dragons) that walk, fly and fall when killed.
- New nature: real trees, pines, dead trees, bushes and rocks, grass, flowers and pebbles that sway in the wind, chosen to fit each level, and textured ground.
- Realistic water: rippling lakes and rivers that reflect the sky and the sun, with lighter shallows and foam along the shore.
- New tower models: stone towers with a ballista, a cannon, ice crystals or a storm orb, growing a floor with each upgrade.
- Difficulty picker in the level menu: Easy, Normal or Hard, remembered between sessions.
