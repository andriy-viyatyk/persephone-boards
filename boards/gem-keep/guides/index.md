# Gem Keep

Gem Keep is a direct-play 3D tower-defense game. Choose an unlocked level and a difficulty from
the level menu, then build and upgrade towers to keep raiders from carrying gems back to their
portals. The starting gold is calculated from the selected level and difficulty.
