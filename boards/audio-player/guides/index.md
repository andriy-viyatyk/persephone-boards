# Audio Player

Audio Player opens audio files, catalog JSON, and M3U or PLS playlists. A catalog and playlist
open in full mode, as does a direct board open with no audio source. A directly opened plain audio
file uses light mode: it keeps playback controls and effects but does not publish `editor.app`.
The full page publishes a model for catalog reads and edits, queue control, and playback.
