      Network

      The username and password are stored in plain text in this board's storage.

      Every tracker, peer, and web-seed connection goes through the proxy, and nothing connects directly if it fails. DHT, uTP, local discovery, and incoming peers are off, so magnet links without trackers may not load, and downloads find fewer peers. UDP trackers need a proxy that relays UDP.

      Trackers and peers are reached directly: the swarm sees your IP address.
