## Goal

Implement the revised `speech.wake@1` contract for [EPIC-139](../../../../persephone/doc/epics/EPIC-139.md): the provider captures and filters audio itself, reports consumer-named phrases, and suppresses computer-sourced speech. Agent Chat (BT-086) is the principal consumer.

## Background

- EPIC-139 decisions 1, 3–6, 8, 9 and 11 define enrollment in each person's voice, stop-word cutting, the shared audio clock, computer-sound suppression and default echo cancellation. Agent Chat uses `agent-wake` and `agent-stop` and chooses sensitivity and microphone.
- US-1783 research selected 96-dimensional embeddings every 80 ms, subsequence DTW and default distance threshold 0.15. The computer-to-microphone delay measured 0.06–0.42 s; missed computer detections can defeat suppression. Its earlier symmetric suppression window is superseded by EPIC-139 decision 4.
- `boards/wake-word/app.js` opens a private service channel and captures with `getUserMedia` and `AudioContext`; `audio-capture-worklet.js` downmixes/resamples to 16 kHz mono s16le in 1,280-sample (80 ms) frames, which the page sends as `audio` then `end`. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain are off. Enrollment records about 2.5 s; `listen` currently accepts all templates and reports stream-relative milliseconds as `type: wake|stop`.
- `scripts/service.mjs` validates 16 kHz input, has a 3 s enrollment limit, a provisional similarity threshold 0.85 (`1 - distance`), 1.5 s per-identity refractory and private page diagnostics. Its energy gate feeds `StreamingFrontend` in `scripts/engine.mjs`; the model uses 76 log-mel frames and 80 ms steps, and `phraseStartSample` estimates a conservative onset. `scripts/enrollments.mjs` atomically serializes `enrollments-v1.json` under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Persephone/board-data/wake-word/`: opaque sample ID, exact case-sensitive phrase/kind/person, time, normalized embeddings and frontend version; old frontend versions are listed but not matched. Grouping and earlier-sample scoring use all three fields. Current page assigns `phrase = kind`; older custom labels also exist. No raw audio is stored. See `doc/tasks/BT-083-wake-word/README.md` for implementation and synthetic checks.
- `board-manifest.json` is 1.0.0, app minimum 5.0.18, bridge 1.42.0, with `microphone`, service, `speech.wake@1` legacy interface and `caller-approval-policy`. `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` describe the current provider; `WHATS-NEW.md` starts at 1.0.0. `model/` contains the ONNX model and Apache license; `vendor/onnxruntime-web/` contains its license and three runtime files. They need no inspection or replacement for this task.
- `C:/projects/persephone/assets/guides/agents/audio.md`: `audio.capture` returns mono 16 kHz s16le 3,200-sample/200 ms `{type:"chunk", epoch, timeMs, sampleRate, data}`; `timeMs` is the first sample on a shared window clock, not arrival time. Microphone captures share by device/default and echo cancellation; computer capture shares globally, emits 400 ms pre-roll and 500 ms tail, and omits gated silence while preserving timestamps. Echo cancellation defaults on and its effectiveness is not guaranteed. A new audio window creates a new, incomparable epoch. `uses: audio@1`, service plus microphone/computerAudio permissions, app 5.0.18 and bridge 1.51.0 are required.
- `capabilities.md` and `boards.md` describe method declarations, `getCapability`, synchronous registration of calls with `persephone.capabilities.handle(id, method, (args, ctx) => ...)`, `ctx.signal`/authenticated `ctx.caller`, and channel opens through `service.onChannel` with `info.interface` and `info.caller`. Channel methods cannot be called by agents; calls take JSON. BT-084's `boards/text-to-speech/board-manifest.json` and `scripts/service.mjs` implement this and consume `audio@1`. Its task verifies that legacy `serviceInterfaces` is still used for discovery/authorization and that explicit methods override same-named synthesized legacy methods; retain the entry, while deliberately rejecting obsolete wire formats. The existing `speech-wake.md` still specifies consumer-owned PCM and stream-relative timing and must be revised in Persephone as US-1784 specifies.
- Device IDs are origin-scoped: the board page cannot supply its own `enumerateDevices()` IDs to the hidden audio window's exact-device `getUserMedia`. `src/main/capability-registry.ts` currently declares only audio `capture` and `play`; `src/main/capability-router.ts` routes those channels to `src/main/audio-service.ts`. In `src/renderer/audio/audio-window.ts`, `requestStreamNow` arms a frame-bound one-use microphone grant, requests a stream, then clears the grant; `src/main/permission-policy-service.ts` expires it after five seconds. This does not establish that labels are available to `enumerateDevices` before a stream is open: return empty labels when unavailable, without opening a stream merely to reveal them. The app's bridge version is 1.51.0 (`src/shared/board-bridge-version.ts`). Bridge 1.51.0 and app 5.0.18 are both unreleased (released v5.0.17 has bridge 1.48.0), so `devices()` ships in the same release as the rest of `audio@1` and needs no bump.

## Concerns / Open Questions

- Gate pre-roll and inference scheduling make a fixed 300 ms wall-clock wait insufficient. Check the selected 900 ms allowance live; the audio contract has no computer-stream watermark, so a delayed computer hit after the deadline cannot suppress an already emitted mic hit. A computer spotter miss also defeats the rule.
- Matching the same phrase across people suppresses a film match even when a person independently says that phrase in the window; this is the intentional decision-4 tradeoff. Template matching is speaker-dependent, so TTS test enrollment must use the same synthetic voice.
- Legacy BT-083 samples were recorded with echo cancellation, noise suppression and AGC off; the audio service enables noise suppression and defaults echo cancellation on. Keep those samples matchable, but changed processing may reduce scores and the user may need to re-enroll. Show which samples predate the change. The provisional 0.85 threshold and conservative onset need real-room tuning by the user.
- Legacy custom phrase labels cannot safely be assigned to a consumer automatically; preserve them and expose their names for explicit reuse. Device labels and the full list may be unavailable until an authorized microphone capture is active; do not claim otherwise.

## Live verification (2026-11-07, Claude via MCP, dev Persephone 5.0.18 / bridge 1.51.0)

No human voice was recorded; the test phrase was spoken by the Text to Speech board through the speakers, and only levels and scores were kept.

- **`audio.devices()` (phase 1) bug, fixed by Claude:** it first returned one placeholder entry (empty id and label) because Chromium hides devices unless the media permission check passes, and the audio window passes it only while main has armed the one-use microphone grant. `src/renderer/audio/audio-window.ts` now arms the grant around `enumerateDevices()`, queued with stream requests so grants never overlap. After the fix: three inputs with ids and labels; capture with a returned `deviceId` delivers chunks.
- **Trust:** the manifest change raised "Board permissions changed"; accepted (own board).
- **Agent surface:** `capabilities["speech.wake"].$help` lists only `describe` and `list`; enroll, delete and listen are hidden from agents.
- **Migration:** 8 `wake` samples → `agent-wake`, 4 `stop` samples → `agent-stop`, marked `legacyCapture` / echo cancellation off; `list("wake")` and `list("stop")` are empty.
- **Enrollment through the audio service:** works, but Text to Speech through the speakers sits at about the 0.009 mean-RMS silence threshold (microphone peak RMS ≈ 0.009), so most test recordings were refused as `silence`; one was accepted. That threshold suits a voice near the microphone.
- **Computer source:** `listen({ phrases:["test-orion"], sources:["computer"], threshold:0.8 })` detected 5 of 5 spoken "Hey Orion" (scores 0.80–0.86) with `time`/`start` on the audio clock (`start` ≈ 1.0–1.1 s before `time`); a non-matching sentence produced no detection.
- **Spurious `reset` fixed by Claude:** `currentEpoch` outlived the captures, so a new listener after the audio window's idle close got `status: reset` at once. `service.mjs` now forgets the epoch when the last capture session closes (`forgetEpochWhenIdle`).
- **Clock alignment:** envelope cross-correlation of a played tone burst on both sources: the microphone lags the computer stream by −5 ms on the shared clock, so the two sources' timestamps line up.
- **Decision 4 (temporary counter, removed afterwards):** microphone-only listener, echo cancellation off, threshold 0.7, six spoken phrases: every strong microphone hit (0.73–0.91) was suppressed; they came 0.5–1.1 s after the computer hit, inside the 1.5 s look-behind. Three marginal hits (≈ 0.70, at the threshold) fired ≈ 0.75 s *before* the computer hit of the same phrase and leaked past the 0.3 s look-ahead. Candidate tunings for the user's TV session: a longer `LOOK_AHEAD_MS` (adds wake latency), or counting computer hits a little below the listener threshold for suppression.
- Test enrollments (`test-orion`) deleted; the user's 12 samples kept. Board page renders: device list from `audio.devices()`, migrated samples marked "Pre-change capture; echo cancellation off". `ui.log` clean.
- Not checked by Claude: enrollment and listening with a real voice, echo cancellation on with real speech, the TV.
